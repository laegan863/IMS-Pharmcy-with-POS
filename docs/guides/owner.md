# Owner: one-page guide

Turn on two-factor authentication the first time you sign in.

**Every morning** you get an email with yesterday's sales, discounts, voids,
refunds, cash over/short and stock expiring within 30 days. The dashboard
shows today's sales, the last 7 days, low stock, near-expiry and expired
stock, and documents waiting for approval.

**People.** _Users_: create staff accounts with a role (owner, pharmacist,
cashier, inventory clerk) and branch. Give supervisors a PIN. Deactivate
leavers; accounts are never deleted so history stays complete.

**Prices.** Only you (and pharmacists) change prices, on the product form.
Every change is kept in the product's price history.

**Settings.** _Store settings_: BIR details, VAT, senior/PWD rules, expiry
alerts, POS controls and numbering. Changes are audit-logged.

**Reports.** Sales by day, cashier and product; gross profit (yours only);
inventory valuation; fast, slow and dead stock; expiry list; statutory
discounts by product and supplier (for RR 7-2026 claims); the BIR sales
summary and sales journal for the accountant. All export to Excel and PDF.

**Audit log.** _Audit log_ shows who did what, when, where and what changed:
sign-ins, sales, voids, refunds, reprints, Z-readings, price and setting
changes. Nothing in it can be edited.

**Backups.** A backup runs every night and is copied off-site. You get an
email if one fails or is more than a day old. Do a restore drill every
quarter (docs/operations/backup-and-restore.md).

**Branches.** Add a branch and its terminals under _Branches_. Each terminal
keeps its own invoice series and a grand total that is never reset.
