# Inventory clerk: one-page guide

**Ordering.** _Purchasing → Reorder list_ shows what is low with a suggested
quantity. Tick the lines, choose the supplier, _Create draft order_. Or start a
_New order_ and scan products in. The pharmacist or owner approves it.

**Receiving a delivery.**

1. Open the approved order, _Receive goods_ (or _Goods receipts → Receive
   without PO_ for a delivery with no order).
2. Enter the supplier's invoice number and date.
3. For every line: the **batch number** and **expiry date** printed on the
   box, quantity, free goods, and unit cost from the supplier invoice.
4. _Post receipt_. If the system warns about short expiry, a price change or
   an expired supplier licence, check it with the pharmacist before posting.

Posting adds the stock. A posted receipt cannot be changed: return extra or
damaged goods with _Supplier returns_.

**Counting.** _Stock counts → Start count_ (one category at a time is easiest).
Count the shelf, type each batch's quantity, _Mark reviewed_. The pharmacist
posts it. Sales during counting are handled automatically.

**Adjustments and disposals.** Broken, lost or found stock: _Adjustments →
New_, pick the batch, type − to remove or + to add, give the reason. Expired
stock: _Disposals_, with the method and a witness. The pharmacist posts both.

**Transfers.** _Transfers → New_, choose the branch and batches, _Dispatch_.
The other branch receives it when the goods arrive.

**New products.** _Products → New product_: generic name, brand, strength,
form, classification (Rx, OTC, S2, non-drug), tax class, the base unit stock
is counted in (tablet, mL, piece) and larger units with how many base units
they hold, and each unit's barcode. You cannot change prices; the owner does.
