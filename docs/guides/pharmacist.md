# Pharmacist: one-page guide

You supervise the counter and own compliance. Turn on two-factor
authentication the first time you sign in.

**Approvals at the counter.** Voids, refunds, price overrides and batch
overrides need your username and **supervisor PIN**. Check the reason before
you approve; your name goes on the record.

**Dangerous drugs (S2).** Only sell against the yellow special prescription
form from a doctor with an S2 licence. Record the prescription with the form
serial number; you approve each sale. Every dispensing goes to the dangerous
drug register automatically. S2 sales cannot be voided at the POS: record any
return as a stock adjustment.

**Prescriptions.** _Compliance → Prescriptions_ lists what is still to be
dispensed. Partial fills are tracked: the balance shows on the prescription.

**Expiry.** The dashboard lists batches expiring within 90 days, and you get
alerts at 90, 60 and 30 days. Move short-dated stock forward, return it to the
supplier (_Supplier returns_), or dispose of it (_Disposals_, with a witness).

**Recalls.** Open the batch (_Stock on hand → product → lot_), _Quarantine this
lot_: it cannot be sold in any branch. The _Recall trace_ on that page lists
every invoice and customer who received it.

**Approve stock documents.** Adjustments, disposals and counts drafted by the
inventory clerk wait for you on the dashboard. Open, check, _Approve and post_.

**Z-reading.** At closing, after every shift on a terminal is closed, run
_Z-readings → Run Z-reading_ and file the printout.

**Books for inspection.** _Reports_: senior citizen record book, PWD record
book, prescription book, dangerous drug register, generic price list. Export
to PDF or Excel. Opening a book with personal data is logged.
