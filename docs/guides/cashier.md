# Cashier: one-page guide

**Start of day.** Sign in with your username. Open _POS_. Count the float in the
drawer, type it in, choose your terminal, _Open shift_.

**A sale.**

1. Scan each item. Scanning the same item again adds one. A box barcode adds a box.
2. No barcode? Type the brand or generic name, arrow to it, press Enter.
3. Change quantity with + / − or type it. Change the unit (tablet, blister, box) in the Unit box.
4. **F9**, type the cash received, **Enter** (or **F12**). The receipt prints; tell the customer the change shown.

**Senior citizen or PWD.** Press **F4**, choose _Senior citizen_ or _PWD_,
type the ID number and the name on the ID, _Apply_. Only one discount per sale.
Ask the customer to sign the receipt.

**Rx items (yellow "Rx" tag).** Click _Attach prescription_. Pick the
customer's prescription, or _Record a new prescription_ from the paper they
hand you. Red "S2" items need the pharmacist.

**Generics.** Click _Generics_ on a medicine line to show cheaper equivalents
and offer them to the customer.

**Card or e-wallet.** Change Cash to Card or E-wallet and type the reference
number from the terminal or app. _Split payment_ adds a second tender.

**Hold a sale.** **F7**, give it a name. **F8** brings it back.

**Mistakes.** Before paying, remove a line with the bin or **Ctrl+Delete**.
After paying, open _Invoices_, choose the invoice, _Void_ or _Refund_. A
supervisor types their username and PIN.

**If the screen says the connection dropped,** do not re-enter the sale.
Press **F12** again when the network is back; it will not charge twice.

**End of shift.** _Close shift_, count the drawer by note and coin, _Close
shift and print X-reading_. Hand the X-reading and cash to the supervisor.

**Never** share your password or let someone sell under your name: every sale
records who made it.
